Easy Curly Hairstyles for College Students: Cute Looks in Minutes

By Lily Bennett | The Freshman Edit

Let's be real: between early classes, late-night studying, and trying to have a social life, spending an hour on your hair isn't always realistic.

Luckily, curly hair can look amazing with just a few simple styling tricks. Whether you're heading to class, studying at the library, or getting ready to go out, these hairstyles are easy to recreate in a dorm room.

5 Easy Curly Hairstyles to Try

ABOUT 2 MINUTES

1. The Curly Pineapple Bun

Perfect for second-day curls or mornings when you hit snooze one too many times.

  1. Gently gather your curls toward the top of your head.
  2. Secure them loosely with a soft scrunchie.
  3. Let your curls fall naturally and adjust any face-framing pieces.

Big sister tip: Avoid pulling too tightly so your curls keep their shape.

ABOUT 3 MINUTES

2. Half-Up, Half-Down Curls

This is one of the easiest ways to keep hair out of your face while showing off your natural curls.

  1. Separate the top section of your hair.
  2. Secure it with a small claw clip or scrunchie.
  3. Fluff the remaining curls for volume.

Best for: Classes, coffee dates, and casual nights out.

ABOUT 2 MINUTES

3. The Claw Clip Updo

A claw clip is a dorm room essential because it can make your hair look styled in seconds.

  1. Gather your hair at the back of your head.
  2. Loosely twist it upward.
  3. Secure with a clip large enough to hold your curls comfortably.

Leave a few curls loose around your face for a relaxed look.

ABOUT 5 MINUTES

4. Two Front Braids with Natural Curls

This style adds a cute detail without needing to style your entire head.

  1. Separate two small sections near the front of your hair.
  2. Loosely braid each section.
  3. Secure the ends with small hair ties.
  4. Refresh the remaining curls with a little water if needed.

Best for: Weekend plans, campus events, and pictures with friends.

ABOUT 3 MINUTES

5. The Low Curly Ponytail

For a simple, clean look, a low ponytail is always a good option.

  1. Create a middle or side part.
  2. Gather your curls loosely at the nape of your neck.
  3. Secure with a satin scrunchie.
  4. Adjust your curls and gently smooth flyaways.

This is especially useful on busy school mornings.

Simple Dorm Room Curly Hair Essentials

You don't need a cabinet full of expensive products. A basic routine can start with:

  • A moisturizing leave-in conditioner suited to your hair
  • A curl cream or lightweight styling gel
  • A spray bottle for refreshing curls
  • Soft scrunchies and a comfortable claw clip
  • A satin bonnet or pillowcase for overnight protection

Big Sister Advice ♡

Don't compare your curls to someone else's. Different curl patterns need different routines, and figuring out what works for your hair takes time.

Some days your curls will look amazing. Other days, a claw clip will save your life. Both are completely normal.

How to Refresh Your Curls Before Class

Start by lightly misting sections that need attention. Gently scrunch your curls and apply a small amount of your usual styling product if needed. Let your hair air-dry while you finish getting ready.

Be gentle with your curls, and avoid piling on too much product. If your hair feels weighed down, use less the next time.

Final Thoughts

Your freshman schedule is already busy enough. Find two or three hairstyles you love and rotate them throughout the week. Looking cute doesn't have to take forever.
