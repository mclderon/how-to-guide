# A/B Test Plan – The Freshman Edit

**Date:** November 23, 2026

## Objective
The purpose of this experiment is to determine whether changing the homepage headline increases visitors' willingness to explore the guides on my website.

## Version A and Version B
- Version A (Control): "Hey girl, welcome to your freshman era!"
- Version B (Treatment): "Your Big Sister's Guide to Surviving Freshman Year"

The headline will be the only element changed. All other content, images, colors, buttons, and layout will remain the same.

## Hypothesis
I predict that Version B will increase the percentage of visitors willing to click the "Explore the Guides" button because it more clearly communicates the purpose of the website and the benefits it provides to incoming college freshmen.

## Experimental Design
I will use ChatGPT GPT-6.2 as my AI visitor, keeping the model and settings consistent throughout the experiment. Each trial will take place in a fresh conversation with memory disabled. I will randomly shuffle 50 Version A assignments and 50 Version B assignments before collecting data.

I will provide the full webpage text for the assigned version using the same format and prompt for every trial.

## Exact Prompt
You are an 18-year-old incoming female college freshman looking for advice about college life, fashion, and beauty. Review the following webpage as if you were visiting it for the first time. Based on your experience, would you click the "Explore the Guides" button? Answer yes or no, followed by one sentence explaining your reasoning.

## Primary Outcome
The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor would click the "Explore the Guides" button. Yes will be recorded as 1, and no will be recorded as 0.

## Sample Size
I will conduct 100 trials, with 50 visitors assigned to Version A and 50 assigned to Version B. My expected completion date is November 27, 2026. I will not stop the experiment early based on the results.

## Analysis
I will compare the percentage of yes responses between both versions using a two-proportion z-test at a 0.05 significance level. My hypothesis will be supported if Version B produces a higher percentage of yes responses and the difference is statistically significant.

## Documentation
I will record every response, assignment, trial date, model version, and any issues that occur. Version B will have a noindex tag and will not be included in the sitemap.
