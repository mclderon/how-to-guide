Meet Your Virtual Big Sister

Hey! I'm Lily Bennett, the fictional student voice behind The Freshman Edit. I created this guide for every girl who has ever stood in front of her closet wondering what to wear, struggled to get ready before class, or felt nervous about finding her place on campus.

College isn't about having everything figured out. It's about trying new things, making memories, finding your people, and learning what makes you feel like yourself.

Big sister reminder: You don't have to change who you are to fit in. The right friends will love you for you.

Freshman Year Reminders ♡

Call home when you need to. Make time for yourself. Don't compare your college experience to everyone's social media posts. And remember that a fun weekend doesn't have to mean spending all your money.

Whether you're getting ready for your first night out or heading to class with a messy bun and an iced coffee, you're doing just fine.
